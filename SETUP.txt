Daily backups are made on first changed sync of each UTC day after an existing cloud save, up to seven backup dates. Restore/import replaces progress; export first. Limitations: client-controlled saves are not anti-cheat, cloud rules are not deployed or live-tested, Google popup may be blocked on iOS standalone mode. No account deletion UI, no public profile directory. Existing browser settings/photo choices remain local. No server-authoritative economy. Firebase usage may incur costs.
